## Hourly rate

- Rate: 25 € per hour
- Minimum project price: 500 €. I don't take very small jobs (50–100 €).
- Website "from" prices: integrations from 500 €, chatbots from 800 €, apps from 1,000 €,
  business systems from 2,500 €. A calculated price is never lower than these.

## Always included (depends on project size)

Every project includes planning, testing, bug fixing and launch. How many hours they take
depends on the size of the project. Pick the row that fits.

| Project size | Example | Planning | Testing | Bug fixing | Launch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | Presentation website, one integration, no database | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Medium | System with log in and a database, a few main features | 3–4 | 6 | 3 | 2 |
| Large | Business system: many roles, integrations, reports | 8–10 | 10–12 | 4 | 2 |

- Buffer for things that come up during the work (unclear requirements included): + 15 %.
- If I have already built something similar (for example a language learning system),
  planning takes less time: ____.

## Timeline

- I work about 20 hours per week on one project.
- Timeline in weeks = total hours ÷ hours per week, rounded up.

## Discounts and alternatives

- Discounts the assistant may offer: 10 % when the whole project is paid upfront.
- The assistant never offers any other discount.
- When the price is too high, it may suggest:
  - a smaller first version (MVP) with only the most important items;
  - building in stages and paying per stage;
  - a simpler solution that takes fewer hours (for example manual entry instead of an API integration).
